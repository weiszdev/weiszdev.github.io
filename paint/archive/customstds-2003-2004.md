# customSTDs.com — recovered from the Internet Archive

Everything below is verbatim from captures of the original site, 2003–2005.
Sources are linked per item. Pulled 2026-08-24.

The runbook assumed this copy was unrecoverable. Most of it was — the 2003 pages
set nearly all their text as **images**, which is why a text scrape comes back
almost empty. The exception is the February 2004 homepage, which has real prose.

---

## Body copy — homepage, 6 Feb 2004

> Look to customSTDs.com for all your custom helmet painting, custom airbrushing,
> and custom motorcycle airbrushing needs. customSTDs.com specializes in custom
> painted helmets and street bike tank and fender painting. We custom paint all
> brands of helmets, Shoei, Arai, HJC, Bell, etc. and all brands of street bikes.
> You name it, we can personalize it for YOU.

Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20040206183456/http://www.customstds.com/

Tagline used sitewide: **Custom Helmet Painting and Airbrushing**

## Tone note for the rebrand

The original brand voice is a 2000s biker-shop register: "taters of the week",
"23 Chick", the dancer-pole clubhouse, EvilAngel pinup commissions. Authentic to
the era and to the shop, and a different posture from a present-day commercial
relaunch. Worth a decision rather than an accident.
